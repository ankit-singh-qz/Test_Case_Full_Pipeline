Title:
Dynamic Pricing and Real-time Seat Management

Description:
As an airline revenue manager, I want to dynamically update flight pricing and seat availability in real-time based on market demand, so that I can maximize revenue, minimize unsold inventory, and improve customer satisfaction by offering competitive prices.

Acceptance criteria:
1. System updates prices within 5 minutes of a change in demand metrics.
2. Seat availability is reflected in real-time within the booking system.
3. Pricing adjustments adhere to predefined revenue management metrics and constraints.
4. An audit log records all pricing and availability changes for review and compliance.

Known constraints (from existing systems, not new requirements):
- Aircraft capacity is already stored per flight and must bound available_seats.
- Existing pricing policy already defines a price floor (cost) and ceiling; pricing adjustments must stay within it.
- If audit logging is unavailable, reject the change rather than applying it without a record.
- Revenue managers are scoped to their assigned routes, not all flights.
- If a revenue manager's session expires mid-update, roll back any partial change entirely; nothing partially applied should ever be visible to downstream systems.
- If two updates for the same flight conflict, reject the later one with a conflict error rather than silently overwriting the earlier one; the caller must re-fetch and retry.

Additional information:
- Client Name: Sabre Corporation
- Module Name: Airline Ticketing
